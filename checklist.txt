# 1 Pre-Departure :: PRE-DEPARTURE (HOME BASE) :: PRE-DEP
## TRIP PLANNING & DOCUMENTS
- Reservation confirmed and conf # saved offline
- Gate code, office hours, and after-hours instructions captured
- Site number and campground map saved to phone
- Campground rules and quiet hours reviewed
- Route checked for bridge height, weight limits, and steep grades
- Weather and wind forecast reviewed for travel day and the stay
- Fuel stops identified with pull-through or truck lane access
- Route and ETA shared with a family contact
- Mail hold, package deliveries, and pet care arranged
- Route grade and water availability at the destination evaluated
- Fresh tank fill level decided for the route
- Insurance card and registration on board
- Roadside assistance number saved offline
- Spare keys on board
- Payment method on board
## SYSTEMS, SAFETY, AND RUNNING GEAR
- Battery state of charge verified and adequate for the drive
- Solar controller reading normal with no fault codes
- Refrigerator powered and down to temperature before loading
- Shore power or generator confirmed working as needed
- Smoke, LP, and CO detectors tested
- Fire extinguisher present with the gauge in the green
- Fresh, gray, and black tank levels noted before departure
- Fresh tank filled to the level decided above
- Trailer tires inspected for wear, cracking, and damage
- Trailer tires set to cold pressure spec
- Trailer spare tire pressure checked and spare accessible
- Lug nuts torque-checked
- Wheel wells and brakes clear of debris
- Exterior lights, seals, and sidewalls inspected for damage
## TOW VEHICLE
- Fuel tank full
- Engine oil and coolant checked
- Washer fluid topped off
- Tow vehicle tires inspected and set to towing pressure
- Tow vehicle spare tire checked and accessible
- Backup and side cameras clean
- Truck bed cargo secured or covered
## PROVISIONING AND LOADING
- Groceries and cooler loaded
- Drinking water loaded
- Medications and first aid kit checked and restocked
- Black tank treatment on board
- RV-safe toilet paper on board
- Grill fuel on board
- Firewood and lighter as needed
- Chairs and outdoor mat loaded
- Outdoor table and site lighting loaded
- Walkie talkies charged and packed in the tow vehicle
- Tool kit and work gloves on board
- Disposable gloves on board
- Duct tape, zip ties, and spare fuses on board
- Sewer hose on board
- Potable water hose on board
- Pressure regulator and filter on board
- Surge protector on board
- Leveling blocks on board
- Wheel chocks on board
- X-chocks on board
- Jack pads on board
- Slide-box exterior storage loaded and contents secured
- Refrigerator loaded tightly and shelves stable
- Bed and dinette cleared
- Bath items stowed
- Loose items put away in their travel places
- Non-camp side slide floor clear of obstructions
- Cargo distributed low and forward of the axles
- Nothing loose in the pass-through or storage compartments
## HOUSE
- Pets loaded or delivered to the sitter
- Thermostat set and house doors locked

# 2 Travel Day :: TRAVEL DAY :: TRAVEL
## GOAL - GET OUT AND LOOK BEFORE MOVING
! Stopped with the parking brake set and both people out
! Walkie talkies on, tested, and on the same channel
! Path walked end to end on foot
! Overhead clearance checked for branches, wires, and eaves
! Ground checked for softness, holes, and stumps
! Obstacles noted: posts, pedestals, spigots, and meters
! Clearance confirmed on both sides at the widest point
! Turn and swing path clear
! Hand signals and a stop word agreed before moving
! Weight distribution bar tension released, bars removed and stowed
## FUEL AND REST STOPS
- Pull-through or exit path confirmed before committing to the lot
- Hub and tire temperatures checked by hand at the first stop and each fuel stop
- Tire pressures spot-checked and TPMS alarms reviewed
- Refrigerator still running and interior temperature acceptable
- Battery state of charge and solar input checked
- Quick look under the trailer for drips or hanging items
- Water and shade for pets
## OVERNIGHT STOP WITHOUT FULL SETUP
- Parked level enough for the refrigerator and for sleeping
- Chocks placed if unhitching
- Jacks down only if needed
- Doors locked and valuables out of sight

## PARK AND SECURE
- Trailer positioned in its storage spot
- Wheel chocks placed front and rear

- Unhitched with the tongue jack on a pad
- Weight distribution bars stowed
- Stabilizer jacks set
## TANKS AND PLUMBING
- Black and gray tanks dumped and flushed if not already done

- Fresh tank drained or left per the storage plan

- Water pump off and system depressurized

- All food and perishables removed
- Trash removed
- Linens and towels removed for laundry
- Floors vacuumed and surfaces wiped

- Windows and vents opened briefly to air the unit out
- Cabinets left slightly open to prevent musty odors
- Pest deterrents placed and entry points checked
## ELECTRICAL

- Battery brought to the storage state of charge for LiFePO4
- Solar controller set for storage
- Inverter off
- Shore power connected or disconnected per the storage plan

- Hoses, cord, and outdoor gear returned to assigned compartments
- All exterior compartments closed and locked
- Roof and seals given a visual check
