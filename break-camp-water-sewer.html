Break Camp: Water, Sewer, and Tank Treatment

Dump, flush, recharge, and disconnect. Same order as the Camp Departure checklist.

Full hookup siteHose already connected. Start at Phase 2. City water stays on until the tanks are recharged.
Dump stationStart at Phase 1. Fill a 3 to 5 gallon container at the site spigot before leaving.
BRING Gloves, sewer hose, flush and wash hose, Kleen Tank jug, 8 oz cup, marked gallon jug, bucket or towel.
OWNER'S GUIDE CORRECTION Connect the dump inlet end first. Remove the RV cap last, with the main dump valve closed.
00

Kleen Tank Solution (Make Ahead)

IngredientAmount
Calgon bath powder (water-softening bath soak)8 oz (heaping cup)
Pine-Sol or similar pine oil cleaner40 fl oz (5 cups)
Warm waterFill to 1 gallon
  1. Confirm the Calgon label says add to bathwater, not a dusting powder
  2. Powder into the empty jug first, then the Pine-Sol
  3. Fill slowly with warm water, cap, shake gently
  4. Let sit overnight, shake again. Label: "HOLDING TANK SOLUTION, DO NOT DRINK"
NOTE Shake before every use. One gallon covers about 5 dump cycles at 24 oz each.
01

Phase 1: Connect (Dump Station)

Full hookup: confirm both ends tight and main dump valve open, then go to Phase 2.

  1. Main dump valve closed CRITICAL
  2. Hose to dump inlet first, tug test
  3. Cap off, hose to RV outlet, tug test
  4. Open main dump valve
02

Phase 2: Black Tank

  1. Flush and wash hose to black flush inlet
  2. Dump gray 1 to wet the hose, then close
  3. Open black fully, 3 to 5 min, until silent
  4. Open flush ballcock, flush until clear (2 to 5 min) BLACK STAYS OPEN
  5. Close flush ballcock, let it drain, close black
  6. Disconnect flush hose
03

Phase 3: Gray 2 and Close Up

  1. Dump gray 2 last to rinse the hose, then close
  2. Black, gray 1, and gray 2 closed CRITICAL
  3. Main dump valve closed CRITICAL
04

Phase 4: Sewer Hose

  1. Disconnect at RV, cap on immediately
  2. Walk the hose up to drain, hold high 30 to 60 sec
  3. Rinse inside with the flush hose until clear
  4. Disconnect at inlet, coil loosely, stow by itself
  5. Gloves off, hands washed CRITICAL
05

Phase 5: Recharge the Tanks

Shake the jug. Dose each tank, then add travel priming water.

TankDoseWherePriming
Black8 ozToilet3 gal
Galley gray8 ozKitchen sink1 to 2 gal
Bath gray8 ozShower drain1 to 2 gal
  • Full hookup: use city water
  • Dump station: black from your container, gray from the fresh tank
  • Boondocking next: 5 gal in black, skip gray
06

Phase 6: City Water (Full Hookup)

  1. Pump off, spigot closed
  2. Potable ballcock opened to bleed pressure
  3. Jumper, filter, and blue hose disconnected and drained
  4. Flush hose drained, regulator and Y removed
  5. Hoses stowed apart
07

Before You Pull Out

CHECK
  • All four valves pushed in and solid
  • Cap tight, no drips
  • Hoses stowed apart, jug secured upright
08

Between Dumps

  • Arrival: 2 to 3 gal in black before first toilet use. Gray 2 to 3 gal, or let use fill it.
  • When to dump: 90 to 100 percent full. Add water first if dumping early.
  • Mid-trip dump: 8 oz per tank plus 3 to 5 gal each.
  • Valves during a stay: Black closed all stay. Gray closed except to dump.
  • Heavy shower use on sewer: Gray may stay open briefly. Close within 30 min.
  • Odor or sensor misreads: Raise the dose in that tank first, usually the galley.
09

Safety

WARNING
  • One tank valve open at a time. Black stays open while flushing.
  • Close black only when silent. If unsure, wait 30 to 60 sec.
  • Cap off only with the main dump valve closed and the hose connected.
  • Push and pull handles straight. The rods bend.
  • Stiff valve: silicone lubricant, never WD-40. Service it if it stays stiff.
  • Sewer, flush, and potable hoses never touch. Replace the sewer hose every 5 to 7 years or at the first crack.
  • Kleen Tank only. No enzyme or digester treatments.
